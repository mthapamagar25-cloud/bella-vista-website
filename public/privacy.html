Booking privacy

Bella Vista uses the information you enter to review and manage your table request and contact you about it.

What we collect

Your name, email, phone number, requested date and time, group size, and any optional requests you provide. Please avoid including sensitive information in special requests. Discuss allergies directly with our team.

How the system works

Booking information is stored on the restaurant’s hosting system and is available to authorised staff. It is not published on the website. We do not use the booking form to sign you up for marketing.

Your private booking link lets anyone holding it check the reservation status and cancel an eligible booking. Keep it private. Staff sign-in uses an essential session cookie. The booking system does not use advertising cookies.

Questions or removal requests

To ask about your information, correct it or request removal, contact info@bellavistalewisham.co.uk or call 020 8922 0037.
